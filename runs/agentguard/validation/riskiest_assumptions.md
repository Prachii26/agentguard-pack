# Riskiest Assumptions Board

**What this is**: every load-bearing, company-level assumption AgentGuard's plan currently rests on, ranked by how much damage being wrong does, with the cheapest test that would decisively confirm or kill each one.

**Why it exists**: `BRIEF.md`, `ASSUMPTIONS.md`, and `strategy/lean_canvas.md`'s ⚠ cell each name risk in isolation, in the document where it happened to come up; nothing until now ranks them against each other. The failure this document prevents: a team that spends its first 90 days of build time (`strategy/gtm.md`) on polish while the one test that could kill the company outright (row 1) sits untested — busy work mistaken for progress.

**How to read it**: row 1 is not a stylistic choice — `BRIEF.md` states plainly that if its gap is not clearly positive, "the entire wedge collapses." Read rows top to bottom as a sequencing constraint, not just a priority list: several lower rows (3, 5, 6) can't be meaningfully tested until product exists, and row 1 is the one test the whole plan is gated on. A skeptic should check that nothing below row 1 is being treated as more urgent in practice.

**Depends on / feeds**: pulls from `ASSUMPTIONS.md` (A2, A3, A4, A6, A9), `strategy/lean_canvas.md`'s ⚠ UVP cell, `strategy/business_model_canvas.md`'s nine hypotheses, `strategy/market_type.md` and `strategy/positioning.md`'s dominant-risk sections, `strategy/sales_roadmap.md`'s saboteur finding, and `BRIEF.md`'s Riskiest assumption section verbatim. Feeds `validation/experiment_board.md` (test detail), `validation/pivot_log.md` (trigger criteria), and — once they exist — `financials/risk_matrix.md` and `narrative/vc_memo.md`.

**Status vocabulary used below**: untested / testing / validated / invalidated. **Every row in this board is currently "untested."** Nothing has run — `ASSUMPTIONS.md`'s Restated hard facts are explicit: zero traction, no implementation exists yet. No row here is marked validated or invalidated, and none should be until a real test actually runs.

## Recommended next 3

1. **Run row 1 before anything else on this board, including before the day-30 free-tier build gate in `strategy/gtm.md` finishes.** It is the only row `BRIEF.md` says collapses the entire wedge if it fails, it is the cheapest test on the board (<$1k, 2 weeks), and every other row's priority is conditional on row 1 not already having killed the company.
2. **Do not let row 3 (competitive window) sit unmonitored while row 1 runs.** It costs nothing and the two threats named (Adversa AI, Microsoft) can close the gap on a timeline this venture doesn't control — start the quarterly check now, not after product ships.
3. **Treat rows 5–8 as explicitly out of sequence** — none of them can be honestly tested before a beachhead motion exists, and forcing them early (e.g., cold-testing edge-high self-serve sufficiency with no beachhead traction yet) would produce noise, not signal. Log this sequencing constraint in `validation/pivot_log.md` so a future session doesn't mistake "not yet tested" for "forgotten."
