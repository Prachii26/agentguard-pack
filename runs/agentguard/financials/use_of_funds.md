## Key hires, in order

1. **Founding engineer #1** — builds the matched-budget aggregator (`tech/deep_dives.md`'s highest-priority, no-reference-implementation component) before anything else, per `tech/not_vaporware.md`'s own Recommended next 2.

## Capital-efficiency metric to report

**Dollars spent per validated riskiest-assumption test outcome** (not ARR, not headcount) — because at this stage, per `validation/riskiest_assumptions.md`, the company's entire value hinges on whether the matched-budget adaptive-vs-static gap holds, and every other metric is downstream of that one answer.

## What the next round's story requires this round to prove

Per `financials/revenue_build.md`'s Stage 2 milestone: the next round's story requires proof that **the free-tier→paid conversion funnel is repeatable and does not depend on founder-led, manual sales effort** — i.e., growth that survives being handed to a non-founder GTM hire. Without that proof, a next round would be raising against a story identical to this one, not a de-risked one.
