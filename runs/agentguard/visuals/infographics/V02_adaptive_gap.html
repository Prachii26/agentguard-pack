Hijack success, same environment, generic vs. adapted attacks

A human red-teaming exercise run jointly by NIST's Center for AI Standards and Innovation and the UK AI Security Institute — government-run, not vendor-sourced.

11%
Generic attacksFixed, off-the-shelf attempts — the static-benchmark condition
81%
Adapted attacksTarget-optimized by human red-teamers — a 7x jump on the identical system
Why this matters for AgentGuard's whole premise: the same defense that looks solid against a generic attack can fail 7x more often against one adapted to it specifically. This is the empirical core of the riskiest assumption in BRIEF.md — and the reason a static benchmark score alone cannot be trusted as a resilience claim.
